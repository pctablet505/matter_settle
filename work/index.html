What It Is

RLAlphaLabs is a reinforcement-learning system for Indian equity markets (NSE & BSE) — research-stage, not a live trading product. It is an independent project published on this domain by its own author, and we point at it because it is the clearest public example of the engineering standard we hold ourselves to.

Why This Instead of a Client Story

We have no client case studies yet — this studio is new, and inventing some was never on the table. What we do have is a real, technically deep system you can open right now: a documented pipeline, published results on held-out data, and a test suite that gates every merge. Judge us on that.

  • 6,300+ automated tests gate every merge; behaviour-sensitive paths (order execution, cost models, feature matrices) are pinned by parity tests.
  • Full Indian brokerage cost model — STT, exchange fees, SEBI charges, GST, stamp duty, plus slippage and market impact. This is the line most "AI trading" work quietly skips, and it is the difference between a plausible result and a fictional one.
  • 700+ stocks tracked across 8 time granularities, 5,000+ data files, six indices — fetched via broker API with quality validation and multi-source fallback.
  • Walk-forward validation across multiple time windows, with results reported on held-out data.

The Performance Numbers, Including the Unflattering One

The published experiment reports a test Sharpe of 0.49 and a 65% test win rate on held-out data, against a validation Sharpe of 1.3. That gap between validation and test is the interesting part: it is what out-of-sample degradation looks like when you measure it honestly instead of reporting the better number. The same record shows that run trades a 20-asset portfolio — the 700+ figure above is data coverage, not the size of that experiment.

Read these as research results, not returns. RLAlphaLabs is research-stage with no live capital deployed, and past simulation results say nothing certain about live performance. We publish them because how a team reports a mediocre number tells you more than the number does.

Honest Status

RLAlphaLabs is in research and paper-trading stages; live deployment is planned, not done. That is a strength for your purposes rather than a caveat to hide: it means the system has been built and validated the way we would build anything for you — walk-forward tested, cost-realistic, gated by measurable criteria before advancing — rather than rushed to a demo.
