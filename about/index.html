About / studio facts

About the studio

Matter Settle is a small AI engineering studio in India. A small team of engineers designs, builds and runs machine-learning systems in production: the data pipelines, the models, the application around them, and the deployment and monitoring.

01 / Facts

What is true today

The facts behind every other page on this site, in one place.

Who
A small team of engineers.
Where
India, working in India Standard Time (IST).
Hours

Builds
Decision systems, data pipelines, the application around a model and LLM features.

02 / A new studio

What a new studio can show you

Matter Settle is new. It has no client case studies yet, and no client logos or testimonials. This site will not invent any.

What you can check instead:

  • The terms we start from, set out before any money moves.
  • A sample pilot scope, with a note on every line.
  • The guides, which show how we think about production systems.
  • A small paid pilot, where you judge the work in your own repository, every week.

03 / What we do not do

Where we say no

  • We do not sell a software product or a platform you have to rent. The work is built in your accounts and belongs to you.
  • We do not take on work where AI is the wrong tool. If a rule, a query or a spreadsheet would do, we say so.
