Privacy Notice

Short and in plain language, because that's what actually gets read.

What this site does with your data

This website has no backend and stores nothing. The contact form does not send anything by itself — it hands the text you typed to your own email app, and the message reaches us through your email provider, not through any Matter Settle server or third-party form service.

There are no analytics, no tracking cookies, and no advertising or marketing scripts on this site.

There are also no third-party requests at all. Fonts, styles, scripts and images are all served from mattersettle.com itself, so loading a page here does not hand your IP address to another company. The one exception is entirely your choice: the links to rlalphalabs.com go to a separate site — run separately from this one — which is only contacted if you click.

The site remembers one thing locally, in your own browser: whether you chose the light or dark theme. That value never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

If you email us, we keep your message in our inbox so we can reply to it. If you'd rather we delete it, ask and we will.

Questions about this notice: settleyourmatter@gmail.com.

Not a substitute for legal advice. This notice describes current practice on a small, static site. It is not a data-processing agreement or a claim of regulatory registration of any kind.
