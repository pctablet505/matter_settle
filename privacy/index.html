Privacy / last updated 2026-10-16

Privacy notice

Short and in plain language. This is what this website does with your data, which is very little.

01 What the site stores

Nothing. This website has no backend and no database. It is a set of plain pages.

The subject and message boxes on the contact page stay in your browser. “Open in my email app” hands the text to your own email app, and the message reaches us through your email provider, not through any Matter Settle server or form service. The site never saves what you type.

02 Analytics, cookies and tracking

There are no analytics, no advertising or marketing scripts, and no tracking pixels. The site's own code sets no cookies.

03 Requests to other companies

Fonts, styles, scripts and images all come from mattersettle.com itself. A page here makes no requests to any other company's servers.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. As with any website, the services that deliver a page receive the technical details your browser sends to load it, such as your IP address. Their own privacy policies cover how they handle those details. Cloudflare also asks browsers to send it a short report when a page fails to load. This site adds no tracking of its own on top.

04 What your browser remembers

One thing: whether you chose the light or the dark theme, kept in your browser's local storage. That value never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

05 Links to other sites

The Elsewhere page links to two outside sites. They are separate sites, run under their own terms, and they are contacted only if you click a link to them.

06 If you email us

Your message stays in our inbox so that we can reply to it. If you would like it deleted, ask, and we will delete it.

Questions about this notice go to the same address: settleyourmatter@gmail.com.

07 What this notice is not

It describes current practice on a small, static website, as of the date at the top of this page. It is not legal advice, a data-processing agreement, or a claim of registration with any regulator.
